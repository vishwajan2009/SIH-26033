## Problem Title
SIH 26033: Multiple intermediaries reduce farmers earnings and increase consumer prices.

## Problem Creater's Organization
Ministry of Consumer Affairs, Food & Public Distribution

## Theme
Agriculture, FoodTech & Rural Development

## Problem Description
Expected Solution: Create a digital marketplace that:

• Connects farmers/FPOs directly with consumers and bulk buyers.
• Provides logistics support.
• Uses AI for demand forecasting and route optimization.

Benefits:

• Better prices for farmers.
• Lower prices for consumers.
• Reduced supply chain inefficiencies.

## Proposed Solution
We propose developing FARM2MARKET, an AI-powered digital agricultural marketplace that connects farmers and Farmer Producer Organizations (FPOs) directly with consumers, retailers, supermarkets, hotels, restaurants, and wholesale buyers.

The platform eliminates unnecessary intermediaries by allowing farmers to upload their agricultural products, specify quantities, set prices, and receive orders directly from buyers.

Key Features:

Direct Farmer-to-Buyer Connection: Farmers can sell their products directly to consumers and bulk buyers without depending on multiple middlemen.

AI-Based Price Recommendation: The system analyses market prices and suggests suitable selling prices to farmers.

Smart Demand Forecasting: AI analyses previous sales, seasonal demand, and market trends to predict future demand for agricultural products.

Smart Logistics Management: The platform connects farmers with available delivery partners and identifies efficient delivery routes.

Product Quality and Transparency: Buyers can view product details, quantity, harvest date, quality information, and farmer details before purchasing.

Multilingual Support: The platform supports regional languages such as Tamil and English to make it accessible to rural farmers.

## Technical Approach
Technologies to be Used:

Frontend: HTML, CSS, JavaScript and Bootstrap.

Backend: Python with Django Framework.

Database: SQLite for the initial prototype and PostgreSQL for future deployment.

Artificial Intelligence: Python, Pandas and Scikit-learn.

Maps and Navigation: Google Maps API or OpenStreetMap.

Payment Integration: Secure digital payment gateway.

Development Tools: Visual Studio Code, Git and GitHub.

Methodology and Implementation Process:

Farmer registration: Farmers create accounts and provide their basic details.

Product listing: Farmers upload product images, quantity, quality details and expected prices.

Buyer registration: Consumers and bulk buyers register and browse available products.

Order placement: Buyers select products, specify quantities and place orders.

AI processing: The system analyses market data and provides price recommendations and demand predictions.

Logistics allocation: Delivery partners are identified based on location and availability.

Order tracking: Farmers and buyers can monitor order status.

Payment: Payments are processed through the integrated payment system.

## Feasibility and Viability
Feasibility of the Idea:

FARM2MARKET can be developed using affordable and widely available technologies. Python Django provides a suitable backend framework, while SQLite can be used for database management during the prototype stage.

The project can initially be implemented in a small group of villages and gradually expanded to other regions.

Potential Challenges and Risks:

Lack of digital awareness among rural farmers.

Transportation and delivery difficulties.

Maintaining product quality during transportation.

Unstable internet connectivity in rural areas.

Building trust between farmers and buyers.

Strategies to Overcome Challenges:

Provide a simple and multilingual user interface.

Collaborate with local transport providers and FPOs.

Introduce product quality verification and packaging guidelines.

Provide SMS notifications and low-bandwidth access.

Implement verified user profiles, order tracking and transparent payment records.
## Impact and Benefits
Potential Impact on Target Audience:

The proposed platform benefits farmers, consumers, retailers, restaurants, supermarkets and agricultural communities.

Economic Benefits:

Farmers can receive better prices by reducing unnecessary intermediaries.

Consumers can purchase fresh agricultural products at competitive prices.

Bulk buyers can directly connect with farmers.

Transportation and distribution costs can be reduced through efficient logistics.

Social Benefits:

Improves farmers' access to digital technology.

Encourages digital literacy in rural communities.

Creates better connections between rural producers and urban consumers.

Supports small and marginal farmers through FPO participation.

Environmental Benefits:

Reduces unnecessary transportation and distribution.

Helps minimize food wastage through demand forecasting.

Encourages efficient delivery route planning.

Supports better utilization of agricultural resources.

Overall Impact:

FARM2MARKET aims to create a transparent, efficient and farmer-friendly agricultural marketplace that improves farmers' income while making agricultural products more accessible and affordable to consumers.
